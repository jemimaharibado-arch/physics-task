A LITTLE LETTER

To my baby,

Happy 18th birthday. 🤍

There are some people who come into your life unexpectedly, and somehow become a part of your favorite memories. You became one of those people for me.

Looking back, it's funny how something that started so simply became something I would eventually care about this much.

From the little conversations, the random chikas, the corny jokes, the tambay sa drum room, and all the ordinary moments in between, somehow they became some of the moments I never want to forget.

And now you're turning 18.

A new chapter, new experiences, new responsibilities, and so many things waiting for you.

I hope you know how proud I am of the person you're becoming.

You don't have to have everything figured out right away. You don't have to know exactly where life is taking you.

Just keep being the person you are. Keep learning. Keep growing. Keep becoming someone you're proud of.

And whenever things get overwhelming, I hope you remember that there will always be someone cheering for you from the sidelines.

Maybe that's one of the things I want this little website to remind you of.

You are appreciated. You are admired. And you are loved in more ways than you probably realize.

Happy 18th, my baby. 🤍

— from someone who will always be
your number 1 fan
